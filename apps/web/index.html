Box each cow in a photo, give her a tag, and the tool suggests who she is next time.

Drop cow photos here or use “Add photos”.

Drag to draw a box around a cow. Click a box to select it; Delete removes it, Esc cancels. Sample herd and photos are drawn demo data.
